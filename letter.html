November 4

Chapter five

A letter from my heart.

It took me months to find the words, and about four minutes to mean them. Read it slowly. It is not going anywhere.

The letter

Handwritten, then typed slowly 04 · 11

My love,

If I could give you one thing today, it would be the ability to see yourself the way I see you. Not only on the good days. On the days you are tired of being strong, on the days you apologise for things that were never your fault, on the days you have decided, quietly and incorrectly, that you are difficult to love.

You are not difficult to love. You are the easiest thing I have ever done.

I know this year has not been gentle with us. There were weeks we both walked around with our hands full of things we did not know how to say, and I know some of those weeks cost you more than they cost me. I am sorry for the parts I made heavier.

But none of it changed the answer to the only question that matters to me. It is you. It was always going to be you. Not because it is easy, but because it is worth it, and because I have never once looked at you and thought there might be someone else I would rather stand beside.

So today, no heavy conversations. Just this: you are loved. Loudly, quietly, on the good days and the ones that hurt. I am not going anywhere. Hold on to us a little longer, because I truly believe everything is going to be alright.

Always, and in every version,
yours.

P.S.

If you ever forget how much you are worth, come back to this page. I will keep saying it until you believe me, and then a little longer than that, just to be safe.

One more thing

There is something waiting for you.
