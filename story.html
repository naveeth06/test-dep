November 4

Chapter three

Our story, in the order it happened.

I have told this story to myself far more often than I have told it to you. Here is the honest version, with all the small things I was too shy to admit I was paying attention to.

The timeline of us

  1. The beginning

    The message I rewrote four times

    I had a whole paragraph ready and sent something much smaller instead. Then I put my phone face down and pretended I was not waiting for it to light up. I was not pretending very well.

  2. The first real laugh

    When you laughed and I forgot my sentence

    Mid-sentence. Completely gone. You did that thing where you cover your face with your sleeve and I thought, quietly, that I would like to be the reason for that sound for a very long time.

  3. The long way home

    Two streets past your door

    We kept walking. Neither of us said anything about it. I remember the cold on my hands and the exact way you said my name when you finally decided it was time to go.

  4. The first hard week

    The night we learned how to stay

    Not the pretty chapter. The one where we were both tired and both right and both wrong. We came out the other side still holding on, which is the part I am proudest of.

  5. The ordinary Tuesdays

    Noodles, one pair of headphones, half a film

    You fell asleep on my shoulder before the ending. I did not move for an hour. If someone had told me then that this was the good part, I would have believed them.

  6. Now

    Still choosing you, on purpose

    Every morning, the same quiet decision. Not out of habit, out of certainty. Of all the people in this world I could have found, it was you. It was always going to be you.

Still my favourite place
is wherever you are.

The good news

The best chapters are still unwritten.

Everything above is only the part I already know by heart. There are pages left: a first apartment, a ridiculous dog, arguments about what counts as a proper Sunday. I want all of it with you.
