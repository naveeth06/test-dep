November 4

Chapter two · the fourth of November

Make a wish, and mean it.

The candles are already lit. Tap each flame when you are ready, then close your eyes and ask for something enormous. I will spend the year trying to meet it.

Your birthday cake

Tap the flames, or press B

Wish received

Now let me spend the year trying to make it real.

I do not know what you asked for. But I hope a little of it was this: more ordinary evenings, more of your laugh in the kitchen, fewer nights spent wondering whether you are loved. You are. Constantly.

The fourth

A day the world got better.

I have never been good with dates. I remember the way a day felt instead. But this one I know by heart, because it is the day the person I love most arrived in the world, and every year I get to be grateful about it out loud.

So: happy birthday. For the cake, for the candles, for the wish, and for all the years of this we still have left to spend.
