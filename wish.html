November 4

Before the last page

Through all of it

I know things have not been easy lately.

We have had our share of difficult moments.

But through all of it, one thing will never change.

How much I love you.

Hold on to us. Brighter days are already on their way, and I truly believe everything will be alright.

No matter what, I am here. You will always be special to me.

Always.

Chapter seven · the fourth of November

Happy birthday, my love.

You made it to another year, and I get to be here for it. That is the whole gift from my side, really: being here. For the candles, for the quiet mornings, for the years that will ask more of us than this one did.

I hope this year is gentle with you. I hope it gives you softness, and sleep, and at least one ridiculous, uncontrollable laugh every single week. And I hope you never spend another day unsure of how loved you are.

Make one more wish

Always, and in every version of this world,
yours.
