This is not really a website. It is a room I built for you, out of the things I never say out loud: that you are the softest part of my life, that the ordinary days with you are the ones I would keep if I could only keep a few, and that in every version of this world, I would find you and choose you again.

There are seven small chapters ahead. None of them are long. All of them are yours. Go slowly. Some of them were difficult to write.
