Chapter four

Reasons I love you, one at a time.

I made a list once. It got out of hand. Twelve of them are on the table, face down. Drag the deck and deal yourself another.

A deck of reasons

01 / 12 ✦ Drag the deck, or let the page scroll it open

The small ones

The things too small to be a whole reason.

  • 01You save me the last bite without announcing it.
  • 02You steal my hoodie and then deny the theft convincingly.
  • 03Your hair in the morning, entirely unbothered by physics.
  • 04You send me a song with no context and it is always right.
  • 05The way you say "come here" when you can tell I am spiralling.
  • 06You get excited about weather. Weather. I find it adorable.
  • 07You remember the version of a story I told once, badly, at 3 a.m.
  • 08You leave your cup in exactly the same place every morning.

If loving you is a habit,
I hope I never get better.
